================================================================================
 PART 4 - Modelling an appointment
================================================================================
 One Appointment class links a patient, a health professional and a time:
   09:00 | Alice Wong (0412 345 678)  | General Practitioner Dr Alice Nguyen (ID 1001)
   09:30 | Ben Smith (0423 456 789)   | Nurse Grace Lee (ID 3001)

 The professional attribute is a HealthProfessional, so the same class accepted
 a GP and a nurse, and printing an appointment shows the professional's details.

 Appointment implements Comparable, so a collection can be put in time order:
   first.compareTo(second) = -1  (negative, meaning the first appointment is earlier)

 The time is stored as a LocalTime, not a String, so comparing and sorting
 times is correct without any extra parsing.
